Shorts Uploader Terms of Service

Last updated 11 November 2026

What this app is

Shorts Uploader is a personal publishing tool. Its owner uses it to upload short vertical videos that they made themselves to their own TikTok accounts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Using the app

  • Only the owner's own TikTok accounts are connected, and each is connected through TikTok's sign-in page with that account's permission.
  • Videos are posted only when the owner chooses to post them. The app never posts automatically without that choice.
  • All content must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The owner is responsible for having the rights to every video, sound and caption posted.

TikTok

This app is not made, endorsed or supported by TikTok. Using TikTok through this app is also covered by TikTok's Terms of Service.

Disconnecting

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Removing it stops all posting from this app.
